# Function Playbooks

Use these as prompts for discovery, not a fixed use-case catalog. Verify each idea against local work, approved data, actual users and failure consequences.

## Engineering, data, IT, platform and cybersecurity

- H1: code/test assistance, query support, documentation, incident synthesis, knowledge retrieval.
- H2: specification-to-test loops, governed analytics pipelines, service-desk triage, deployment or remediation assistance with approvals.
- H3: intent-driven platforms, self-improving developer experience or adaptive operations.
- Measures: lead time, defect escape, recovery time, change failure, analyst cycle time, support resolution.
- Watchouts: secrets, insecure code, fabricated APIs, production access, alert amplification and unclear rollback.

## Product, design, research and innovation

- H1: evidence synthesis, concept variants, interview preparation and prototype support.
- H2: continuous discovery loops linking feedback, hypotheses, prototypes, evaluation and roadmap decisions.
- H3: adaptive products, AI-native experiences or new research capability.
- Measures: discovery cycle, experiment quality, usability, adoption, learning velocity and outcome movement.
- Watchouts: synthetic evidence mistaken for customers, rights/provenance, accessibility, manipulation and weak evaluation.

## Sales, marketing, communications and customer success

- H1: approved-source account research, draft content, call preparation, theme and sentiment analysis.
- H2: governed lead/account workflows, content operations, service summarization and human-owned next-best-action.
- H3: new customer experiences or continuously adaptive go-to-market systems.
- Measures: conversion, cycle time, content performance, retention, service quality and complaint rate.
- Watchouts: privacy, spam, deceptive personalization, unsupported claims, discrimination and autonomous commitments.

## Finance, accounting, audit and corporate development

- H1: variance narratives, scenario support, reconciliation assistance, memo drafting and diligence organization.
- H2: controlled close, forecasting, exception and decision-support loops with source traceability.
- H3: adaptive resource allocation or real-time decision systems.
- Measures: close time, forecast error, control exceptions, analysis cycle, cash or margin impact.
- Watchouts: model risk, stale data, material misstatement, segregation of duties and unverified external facts.

## People, HR, talent and learning

- H1: policy navigation, job and learning drafts, de-identified feedback synthesis and manager preparation.
- H2: employee-service, onboarding and skill-development loops with transparency and human review.
- H3: skills-based workforce systems or redesigned talent operating models.
- Measures: time to proficiency, service resolution, mobility, learning transfer, employee experience and fairness indicators.
- Watchouts: employment decisions, surveillance, protected attributes, explainability, works councils and confidential data.

## Legal, compliance, risk, privacy and assurance

- H1: source-grounded research, document comparison, issue spotting and control description.
- H2: obligation mapping, contract review assistance, control monitoring and triage with accountable sign-off.
- H3: continuous assurance or policy-aware workflows embedded in operations.
- Measures: review time, issue detection, false-positive rate, remediation cycle and coverage.
- Watchouts: privilege, jurisdiction, outdated authority, unauthorized advice, independence and false confidence.

## Operations, supply chain, procurement, manufacturing and field service

- H1: SOP support, planning analysis, handover, supplier research and maintenance knowledge retrieval.
- H2: exception-management loops connecting forecasts, inventory, schedules, inspections and escalation.
- H3: adaptive operations, intelligent networks or semi-autonomous service delivery.
- Measures: throughput, service level, downtime, waste, inventory, safety and first-time fix.
- Watchouts: physical safety, labor impact, supplier confidentiality, stale procedures, resilience and human override.

## Program, project and transformation management

- H1: planning drafts, dependency extraction, status synthesis and risk prompts.
- H2: evidence-linked portfolio monitoring, decision logs and scenario-based replanning.
- H3: continuous strategy-to-execution systems.
- Measures: decision latency, predictability, benefit realization, risk closure and stakeholder effort.
- Watchouts: polished summaries hiding uncertainty, false precision and automation replacing sponsor decisions.

## Health, science, regulated research and care operations

- H1: literature synthesis, non-diagnostic administration and reviewed communication.
- H2: governed research, operational or care-support loops with professional oversight.
- H3: new evidence-generation or service models after validation and authorization.
- Measures: quality, access, turnaround, protocol adherence, safety and professional workload.
- Watchouts: patient/participant data, clinical claims, bias, validation, consent and jurisdiction-specific rules.

## Education, public sector and nonprofit services

- H1: plain-language content, lesson/support drafts, research and case summarization.
- H2: accessible service, case-support or learning loops with human accountability.
- H3: new models of public value, personalized learning or proactive service delivery.
- Measures: access, completion, learning, case time, equity, trust and cost to serve.
- Watchouts: due process, vulnerable people, accessibility, records rules, procurement, transparency and digital exclusion.

## Executive, administrative and knowledge-management work

- H1: briefing, meeting synthesis, search, drafting and decision preparation.
- H2: trusted knowledge workflows, action tracking and evidence-linked decision cycles.
- H3: new management systems that shorten sensing-to-action loops.
- Measures: decision quality and latency, rework, search time, follow-through and knowledge reuse.
- Watchouts: confidential material, hallucinated consensus, loss of dissent and weak records.

## Mixed or uncommon functions

Build a local playbook from six fields:

1. outcomes owned;
2. recurring work and decisions;
3. artifacts and systems;
4. scarce judgment;
5. failure modes and affected people;
6. measurable friction.

Then derive one H1, one H2 and one H3 possibility. Do not force H3 if the evidence supports only assistance or redesign.
