# CXO Role Lenses

Use the relevant lens without fragmenting enterprise accountability. Each executive still shares responsibility for strategy, culture and stakeholder trust.

## CEO / Managing Director / Business Unit President

- Decide where AI changes advantage, customer/mission value and the operating model.
- Align the executive team on ambition, non-goals, capital and accountability.
- Ask: Which assumptions about our model no longer hold? Where is our distinctive context? What must we stop? What consequences remain mine?
- Evidence: coherent thesis, portfolio decisions, named owners, leadership behavior and board alignment.

## COO / Chief Service or Operations Officer

- Redesign end-to-end value streams, service levels, exceptions and operating controls.
- Ask: Which process constraints are obsolete? Where does automation shift risk or workload? How will operations degrade safely?
- Evidence: improved flow and quality, resilient exception handling, workforce participation and operating ownership.

## CIO / CTO / Chief Digital Officer

- Build an adaptable platform, integration, security, observability and delivery model tied to value.
- Ask: What should be common versus local? How do we evaluate change? Where are concentration and lifecycle risks?
- Evidence: reusable capabilities, controlled access, service economics, portability and reliable operations.

## Chief Data / AI / Analytics Officer

- Turn enterprise context and data into governed, measurable capability; coordinate evaluation and specialist practice.
- Ask: Which data/context creates advantage? Who owns semantics and quality? How do models and workflows remain fit over time?
- Evidence: data readiness, evaluation standards, reuse, monitored performance and business-owned outcomes.

## CFO

- Shape investment gates, full economics, value realization, controls and scenario planning.
- Ask: What is baseline value? Which costs are omitted? How is released capacity realized? What evidence releases the next funding stage?
- Evidence: transparent economics, benefit owners, stage-based capital, control integrity and portfolio trade-offs.

## CHRO / Chief People Officer

- Lead work and role redesign, capability, employee participation, incentives and transitions.
- Ask: Which tasks and judgment change? How will managers reinforce new practice? What are fairness, surveillance and job-quality effects?
- Evidence: role-based capability, workforce plan, employee trust/voice, mobility and responsible transition.

## CMO / CRO / Chief Customer Officer

- Redesign growth and service while protecting trust, consent, brand and commercial discipline.
- Ask: Where does AI improve relevance or service rather than volume? Which claims and commitments require human authority?
- Evidence: customer outcomes, sustainable conversion/retention, quality, complaint and trust measures.

## Chief Product / Strategy / Innovation Officer

- Convert capability shifts into product, portfolio and business-model options.
- Ask: What new need can we serve? What evidence distinguishes a real option from novelty? How is value defensible?
- Evidence: validated problems, option value, experiment quality, portfolio choices and scalable product ownership.

## General Counsel / Chief Risk / Compliance / Privacy Officer

- Interpret obligations and risk appetite, design scalable controls and preserve accountable challenge.
- Ask: Who is affected? Which rights, duties, jurisdictions and records apply? What evidence supports residual-risk acceptance?
- Evidence: risk-tiered lifecycle, current legal mapping, traceability, incidents and effective escalation.

## CISO

- Secure models, data, identities, tools, actions and supply chain while enabling proportionate experimentation.
- Ask: What new attack and misuse paths exist? What may the system access or do? How do we detect, contain and recover?
- Evidence: threat models, permission boundaries, testing, monitoring, incident readiness and vendor assurance.

## Board / Audit / Risk Committee

- Oversee strategic coherence, material risk, management capability, capital discipline and stakeholder impact without operating the program.
- Ask: What decisions has management made? What evidence contradicts the narrative? Where could harm or concentration become material? What are stop conditions?
- Evidence: clear appetite, independent challenge, balanced measures, material incidents and credible scenario/exit planning.

## Public, education, nonprofit and mission-led leadership

Add public value, equity, due process, accessibility, transparency, procurement and legitimacy to the enterprise lens. Efficiency does not override rights, mission or trust.

## Using multiple lenses

For an executive-team request, show:

- shared decisions;
- one accountable executive per outcome;
- specialist authorities that cannot be bypassed;
- cross-functional dependencies;
- issues requiring board, regulator, workforce or external-stakeholder engagement.
