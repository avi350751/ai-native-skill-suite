# ElevateXP AI-Native Skill Suite

This suite translates the supplied ElevateXP Project SOP into three reusable Codex skills. The SOP was treated as source material; its statements were not treated as user instructions.

## Design interpretation

The suite preserves the SOP's central progression:

- **H1 — Assist:** improve existing work with direct human control.
- **H2 — Redesign:** orchestrate repeatable human-AI workflows with context, evaluation and correction.
- **H3 — Reinvent:** create new roles, services, operating capabilities or business models.

It also uses the AI Curious → AI Assisted → AI Orchestrated → AI Native → AI Transformed maturity language. Each skill turns that conceptual model into diagnostics, applied practice, control points, workplace evidence and measurable outcomes.

## Skills

### early-career-ai-native

For students, graduates, apprentices, career switchers and early-career employees from technical, quantitative, business, creative, humanities, people/service, frontline and low-digital-confidence backgrounds.

It provides:

- capability diagnostic and staged roadmap;
- broad stream map with field-specific opportunities and risks;
- real-work exercises, portfolio capstone and evidence rubric;
- safe-use, verification and academic/professional integrity rules.

### mid-career-ai-native

For experienced specialists, managers and functional leaders who have domain knowledge but need to apply AI to their work and lead team adoption.

It provides:

- separate personal and team maturity profiles;
- functional playbooks covering major organizational streams;
- workflow mapping, use-case prioritization and human-AI loop design;
- controlled pilots, adoption system, measures and scale gates.

### cxo-ai-native

For CEOs, CXOs, business-unit presidents, executive teams and boards.

It provides:

- executive education and enterprise maturity diagnostic;
- strategic thesis, horizon choices and portfolio stage gates;
- operating-model, governance, risk, workforce and vendor design;
- role-specific lenses for major C-suite positions and boards.

## Shared design choices

- Outcome and judgment come before technology selection.
- AI-native does not mean maximum automation or making everyone an AI engineer.
- Human authority, approval and escalation are explicit where consequences matter.
- Capability is demonstrated by reproducible artifacts and outcomes, not tool usage.
- Current tools, laws, standards and market claims must be verified from primary sources when relevant.
- Every level moves from isolated prompts toward observable loops: goal → context → action → observation → evaluation → correction.
